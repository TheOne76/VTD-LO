Track voxel stability

Separate corner and surface voxel states summarize repeated map observations through online mean and variance updates. Stability follows observed geometry rather than semantic object labels.

Maintain useful constraints

Voxel-temporal states guide correspondence screening during registration and the removal of unstable geometry when updating the local feature map.

Weight retained geometry

Temporal stability, map-point age, and measurement range contribute to bounded residual weights used with robust nonlinear pose optimization.
